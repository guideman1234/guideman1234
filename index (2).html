Read this first

  • This is one player's personal plan, built on Taiwan/Korea experience and the Limited Sample Test (LST) build. Global may differ.
  • Some figures, such as feather counts, were unclear in the source. Check them in game.
  • Item level (GS) is the gate for content. It is not the same as combat power.
Mindset

It is a journey, not a race

The creator has played 11 months and wants to reach endgame fast. He stresses you do not have to.

  • You cannot break your character. If you do quests and dungeons, you will make gains.
  • Reaching the Season 1 raid in week 2 vs week 5 does not put you far ahead. The main edge is a bit more pity progress.
  • Min-maxing everything is not required and not always rewarded.
  • Treat every video (including this plan) as a blueprint, not gospel. Enjoy the story, or min-max, or make gold, as you like.
The six rules behind the plan
1

Energy is king

Odyle Energy claims dungeon rewards. Every point counts, so unlock it early on every character.

2

Spend energy only on the latest stage you can enter

Skip leveling dungeons and earlier stages. Open exactly 3 boxes (not 4) on exploration dungeons to claim one guaranteed piece.

3

Alts feed the main

Alts run low-tier dungeons for kina, crafting materials and dungeon gear. All of it goes to the main.

4

Do not spend kina on temporary gear

Enhancement stones come back when you dissolve gear. The kina does not. Only upgrade your belt and amulet.

5

Skip open-world PvP and PvP gear

Do only the time-sensitive weekly PvP events. Spend AP on stigma shards.

6

Soulbinds later, except for game feel

They add power, not item level. Only chase movement speed and combat speed early.

Why item level matters

Item level does not equal power. It is the gate to higher content. Early on you chase item level to enter content; power comes later.

Odyle Energy in one page

What it does

Spent at the end of dungeons to claim rewards. Builds per character once the Expedition menu is unlocked.

Why level 22 first

The mission at level 22 unlocks the Expedition menu, which starts the energy build. Crow Cave is often quoted at level 20, but the menu needs 22.

You do not have to beat Crow Cave

Only unlocking the menu matters. Energy starts building right away, so unlock it on every character as early as possible.

Day 1, part 1

Level 22, then alts

1

Create your main

You get four free character slots at the start. The plan uses the main plus three alts.

2

Main to level 22

Complete the level-22 mission to unlock the Expedition menu (not just the dungeon door). Energy starts building.

3

Log off, make an alt, repeat

Take each alt to 22 the same way. This front-loads energy on all four characters.

4

Then push the main to 45

Follow the main story quest. Handle exploration content only as it appears on your route.

Alt creation rule

You cannot create a character on a server that is full for creation unless you already have one there. If you do, you can make alts even when it is locked. The creator expects stable early servers.

Ascension quests

During the main quest you hit "ascension quests" that block progress. To start one, fill the ascension bar (top right) using exploration content. That is why you still do seal dungeons and green quests on the way, but do not be a completionist while leveling alts.

Day 1, part 2

Feathers and the reasons to hit 45

Feathers: only on your main

  • The monolith is server-based. Collect on your main, then press the sync button to give feathers to alts. Ignore feathers on alts.
  • Roughly 186 feathers are needed to max the monolith (the source also mentions about 560 in inventory; the figures are unclear).
  • Extra feathers can become power shards.

Why reach 45 on day 1

Duties

The only daily type. Unlocks at 45 and resets daily. Grab and do them immediately. Prioritize petals and cube keys.

Nightmare

Solo boss content that pays currency for the trade shop. Tickets build at 2 per day, so earlier 45 means an earlier final boss, Colossus, which gives big stats into season 2.

Lower Abyss

Unlocks at 45 with its own feathers and monolith. It gives PvP stats (useless for you) but also points on the PvP Daevanion board, and each board level adds item level. All the Abyss feathers are worth up to +52 item level.

The Abyss is PvP-enabled

Crowds appear fast after launch. The earlier you enter, the fewer players. If the vibes are bad, drop it and come back on day 3 (or at night).

Day 1 checklist

  • Main and 3 alts at level 22 (Expeditions unlocked)
  • Main at 45, duties done
  • Nightmare tickets started
  • Tried Abyss feathers (skip if crowded)
  • Spare time: alts to 45

Streamer-event note: if an event pushes you to reach 45 first, the creator may rush the main to 45 before making alts. It costs a little energy, nothing major.

Day 2

Exploration on every character

Finish alts to 45, then clear seal dungeons, strongholds, feathers and green quests on all four. This locks in power, Daevanion crystals and item level.

  • This is slow. You may not finish all four characters in one day.
  • Main collects all feathers, then syncs the monolith to alts.
  • Collect upgrade materials for the belt and amulet on the way.

How to morph

Use the Substance Morph menu, Gear tab, to upgrade to the next grade after +10. Repeat until gold.

Do not enhance gold

Too expensive early for too little gain. Settle on gold baseline.

Clash Runes: go to +1 and stop

Each enhancement lowers the success rate and a failure breaks the item. Push Clash Runes to +1 for the item level. If you find more, you can push them further later.

Opposite faction map: likely skippable

On Taiwan and Korea the rift dungeons on the other faction's map gave the same rewards, so you had to clear them. On the LST, the Daevanion crystals and skill points were removed from those dungeons. If global matches, you do not need to cross the rift. If they return, you will.

Day 2 checklist

  • Alts at 45
  • Seal dungeons, strongholds, green quests done on all characters
  • All feathers collected on main and synced
  • Belt and amulet at gold; Clash Runes at +1
Day 3

Abyss feathers, then endgame

Back to the Abyss

Exploration will likely bleed into day 3. When done, return to the Abyss and check if crowds have thinned. If not, go at night. These feathers are essential for the item level needed to reach late content early.

What "endgame" means here

From now on the loop is: alts run dungeons, main spends energy on the latest stage possible, crafting runs while you are away, and item level climbs step by step.

Crafting: your AFK engine
  • Queue a large batch of crafts at once. They run back to back.
  • Each craft gives a flat amount of XP by craft level, and that does not shrink as you level. So spamming the level-1 craft to max is legitimate.
  • Level-1 crafts need almost no materials: buy the NPC or white basic ingredient (or from gatherers on the auction house) and send it.
  • Whenever you go AFK or to sleep, queue crafts.

Profession levels

Novice 1

Spam the level-1 craft with basic ingredients.

Novice 50

A quest asks you to craft any item. Doing it lets you break into Professional.

Professional 20 to 30

Reach 20 with level-1 ingredients, then bulk-craft blue Baelic (Balaur) materials to reach about 25 to 30 before crafting accessories.

Why level first

Crafting can fail. Success chance goes up with craft level, so level as high as practical before starting the expensive crafts.

Why crafted gear is worth it on global

  • Global crafted gear has no baseline PvE bonus like Taiwan/Korea, but it has extra soulbind slots. Every piece gets a base amount of lines, so crafted pieces are better.
  • After the first craft you just upgrade the same piece as new patches add materials.

Each step has a proc chance: heavy RNG, slow and expensive early. The alternative is to buy gold bases from the auction house with kina made by your alts. The creator's guess is around 4 million kina (it is unclear whether that is per piece).

Failed procs are not trash

If a craft does not proc, keep it. These pieces are used in supply deliveries, so list them on the market or turn them in. Waiting also lowers auction prices as more crafters appear. If bases are too scarce, craft the chain yourself.

Endgame structure

Alts feed the main

Content is split by star tier: conquest dungeons at 1, 2 and 3 stars, and Transcendence (1 star, split into stages that need more item level and pay more).

Main's job

  • Enter the latest stage without wasting energy on earlier ones
  • Prioritize content with guaranteed gains
  • Prefer 3-star over 2-star over 1-star dungeons

Alts' job

  • Run low-tier stages for kina and mats
  • Get specific dungeon gear for supply requests
  • Later: Shugo Festival for stigma shards

Do not open boxes in the main story dungeons

Those boxes only give low-level trash gear, so save the energy. The exceptions are the exploration dungeons at 45 (next pages).

Not in the 1,900 plan yet?

If you cannot enter Stage 2, run Stage 1 for gains first. Then move up to Stage 2 to collect cards.

Steps 1 and 2

Vakron and quest gear

1

Vakron exploration, 3 boxes

At 1,400 item level (you will be near 1,500 after exploration content). Open the end box three times to claim one guaranteed piece. Take the chest piece. Cost: 120 energy.

2

Alts run dungeons, main crafts

Feed kina and mats to the main. Buy or craft gold bases and make the crafted necklace, earrings and rings, then the weapon.

3

Reach 1,900 with crafted gear

Once accessories and weapon are crafted and quest gear is upgraded, you should be near the Stage 2 requirement.

Transcendence Stage 2: what to expect

  • Guaranteed green Arcana cards
  • Chance at the chalice
  • Possibly around a 50% chance for blue cards
  • The first content you farm on your main after the exploration stops

Arcana in Season 1

One dungeon drops the bell and compass; the other drops the parchment and mirror. The chalice comes only from higher levels. Just fill the slots. Do not farm for specific set effects or skills: you will replace them.

Steps 3 and 4

At 2,100 with crafted gear and Arcana in place. Cost again 120 energy. Do not claim the piece yet: you will pick it later based on how your conquest luck goes. The boxes can still drop a rare item and better blues.

Run Ferocious Horn Den (Tier 3 conquest) 28 times

This dungeon drops the Nuakum gear: the armor and the guard that your crafted weapon and accessories do not cover.

Stage 4 and the Sanctuary

5

Even without a full set you will have enough item level. Now farm the proper Arcana for the skills and passives your class needs.

Weekly energy top-up

Each week, convert Odyle energy in the Substance Morph menu and buy the sub-shop energy. The creator says this is about 20 full double-claim dungeon runs. The battle pass also adds energy.

Daily and weekly systems

Do these late in the week

Performance-based content

Daily dungeon, Nightmare and Ascension Trial rewards scale with your performance. The creator runs them on the last day of the week, when the main is strongest, even on alts (they gain gear from dungeons too).

Ascension Trial

Character-based. Gives enhancement stones, mana stones and pet souls. Run as late as possible in the week.

Daily dungeon

Also gives petals on each character. This is why you barely need to farm pets.

Supply requests

Daily and weekly quests that pay AP (uncapped, precious) and enhancement material. Do as many as you can without breaking the bank. Buying items to turn in is usually too expensive for the AP.

Request typeAdvice
Dungeon gearSend an alt to run the dungeon for that piece, then pass it to the main through the warehouse.
Crafted gearWorth doing. Extras and failed procs sell well on the market.
Refining stonesWorth turning in.
Odyle, raw leather, etc.Skip early: expensive, and AP reward is small.

World bosses (open map)

Give boxes with loot you can turn in on the supply tab for more AP. That tab resets each season and the bosses take long to kill early, so do not rush them.

Before the first weekly reset (assumed Wednesday)

  • At 45, buy the weekly contracts from the command merchants: they carry over to the next week.
  • Convert Odyle energy in Substance Morph and buy sub-shop energy.
Shugo Festival and Invasion

Wait for max level

  • Keys regenerate 3 per day per server. They are server-bound, so run them on your main.
  • Rewards are level-bound, so do not start 'til you reach max level.
  • Not time-sensitive: keys cap out after about a week.

What it gives

  • Odyle and artisan stones (your main source of radiant and pure Odyle)
  • Tokens for the festival shop
  • Stigma shards (new on global)

Shop priority

  1. Daevanion crystals (empty the category)
  2. Stigma shards
  3. Radiant Odyle only if a craft is blocked

Shugo mini-game trick

Rewards depend on ranking. Finish outside the top two and you get nothing, so leave the event. On the LST one mini-game worked like this, and everyone reached first or second:

Gathering: skip it early

  • It is time-consuming but mandatory for crafting. Best used to sell to players who skip it.
  • Leveling mirrors crafting: Novice to 50, gather a specific node for the quest, then Professional to 50.
  • No energy system: gather as long as you like.

Gathering skill points

  • Left side (passives): raise the success gauge and reduce fail buildup.
  • Right side (node specializations): see nodes on the minimap; double-proc; chance to upgrade mat tier.
  • Focus Odyle first (it morphs into everything), then something convenient such as herbs.
  • Skills reset cheaply, so do not level gathering on alts.
Pets, Genus Insight and kina

Ignore at first, then set up

These give power, not item level, so they are not the early priority.

Pets

  • Collect pets on the way while leveling
  • Daily dungeons on each character give petals, so leveling needs little farming
  • Some pets cannot be farmed (story bosses, dungeon bosses): snipe them from the Black Cloud merchant

Genus Insight

  • Streamlined on global
  • Cube keys from the monolith on 2 characters can bring all to level 10
  • Gives many rainbow crystals, then grind for stats
  • Stats differ from Korea: the creator plans a separate video

Open-world kina farming

  • Grind mobs up to a daily cap of about 1 million kina. After that, loot is restricted.
  • Gold drops from mobs sell well early on the auction house, but the creator advises keeping them for soulbind and transfer material.

Where not to spend kina while leveling

  • Do not enhance gear you will replace.
  • Dissolving returns enhancement stones but not kina, and kina is scarce early.
  • The only exception: belt and amulet.

Season shop materials

The season shop's materials tab (Odyle, draconic ingredients) is mostly bound, but on the LST it could be moved to server storage. Buy these on alts and keep the main's tokens for the Season 1 base wings (Talisra Wings).

Ping

A network patch made ping much less of an issue. Even 100 to 150 ping costs a bit of damage but nothing huge. Dodging is the main thing affected. You can play any class.

Soulbinds

The biggest early pitfall

Soulbinds add power, not item level. Do not hunt for them early. Only reroll for game feel:

Once you have a crafted weapon

  • Use soul codexes (quest rewards, Twitch drops) in the Reset tab on the weapon.
  • Keep rerolling 'til you have two usable stats, or combat speed, or a rare line like damage boost.
  • Then repeat on the necklace and earrings.
PvP and Abyss Points (AP)

Skip

  • Open-world PvP: early on everyone is an unranked trainee, ~300 AP per kill
  • PvP gear: Kanis gear is only ilvl 62 and costly in AP; Star/True Dragon crafted is ilvl 70

Do

  • Weekly and timed events: war bosses, sieges, battlegrounds (medals and bonus AP)
  • Join sieges and world bosses via LFG: presence alone gives most weekly AP
  • Abyssal corridors if your faction wins artifacts

Spend AP on stigma shards

10,000 AP each on global. Some classes do not function without specific skills at specific levels. AP from hunting is cumulative, so you can catch up later.

Battle passes

One Odyle (energy) pass and one cosmetic pass. Buy both on the main, and the energy pass on alts too if allowed.

Making kina on the auction house

Players like the creator burn steps by buying from others. You earn kina by slowing your own progression a bit. Ideas:

  • Gathering: sell ingredients to crafters who want to level fast.
  • Artisan bases: sell gold bases at a premium to players skipping the chain.
  • Kina to token conversion: turn kina into boxes, sell them for the premium currency, then use that for battle passes or to rebuy kina later when the price falls. Early on, buying kina is poor value (about $50 for 2M kina).

Trade-offs and limits

  • Selling your kina slows your own gear, so it is a long-term play.
  • No cross-server trading: the world market is expected to stay closed for all of Season 1.

Stats and alt classes

  • All classes go offense, no exception. See the creator's PvE building guide for details.
  • Alt classes do not matter much: pick ones you like, gear them with dungeon gear, maybe cheap crafted accessories later.
  • Realistic ring goal: two main skills at level 20 (three were easy on Korea, but global rings are busier).
Q&A quick answers

Common questions

Do I have to beat Crow Cave at 22?
No. Unlock the Expedition menu and energy starts building.
Where do I spend energy?
Not on leveling dungeons. Exactly 3 boxes on the latest exploration dungeon, then the highest stage you can enter (Transcendence), preferring 3-star over 2- and 1-star.
Is crafted gear good for PvE on global?
Yes, thanks to extra soulbind lines and easy upgrading over time. No baseline PvE bonus, unlike Taiwan/Korea.
Is buying PvP gear worth it early?
No. It is ilvl 62 vs 70 for crafted, and AP is better spent on stigma shards. PvE gains come first.
How do I get AP without PvP?
Shugo Festival, supply deliveries, duties, weekly contracts, joining sieges and world bosses through LFG, and abyssal corridors.
How do I spend kina while leveling?
Nowhere, except belt and amulet upgrades.
Best weekly prep before the first reset?
Buy weekly contracts at 45 (they carry over) and claim all weekly energy from Substance Morph and the sub shop.
Should I kill world bosses early?
Not urgently. Open-world ones feed supply AP but reset seasonally. Abyss bosses are timed, so do them if you PvP.
Best way to gear alts?
Dungeon gear from their own runs. Optionally cheap crafted accessories. Later, Shugo Festival for stigma shards.
Which class suffers least from ping?
Ping mainly affects dodging. After the network patch you can play anything.
Are some Shugo games faster?
You need a top-two finish. See the orb-and-tiles trick in the Shugo Festival section.
Does gathering have an energy limit?
No. Do not level extra node types on alts.
Cross-server trading at launch?
No. The world market is closed, probably all of Season 1.
Can I max Genus Insight early?
Yes: level 10 for all of it using monolith cube keys from two characters. Keys are more plentiful on global.
Where do draconic ingredients come from?
Conquest dungeons: gold ones and also blue ones.
